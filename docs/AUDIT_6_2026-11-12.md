## Verdict

The research pipeline meets the master specification's acceptance criteria (section 19), with the gaps stated below.
Today's work adds what the owner could not see or use: a one-click dashboard launcher, a 1-5 stock comparison tab,
levels with reasons in every trade view, and a single notebook that trains and evaluates every model in the open.
The forward test missed one session (2026-11-10, incident D-052 below); the next was made up on time.

The evidence has not changed and the notebook shows it again:
* **Supported:** volatility regime, cross-sectional rank (small), calibration, a 20-session market read.
* **Not supported:** single-stock direction at 1-20 sessions, value from charts, news and filings beyond the
  indicators, and an economic edge over buy-and-hold.

**Compare 1-5 (new).** Pick up to five stocks and a logged session. The tab shows:
* a card per stock and a one-paragraph verdict;
* price paths rebased to 100, with drawdowns;
* v1.1/v2 probabilities and the 10-90% return ranges at the chosen horizon;
* the term structure of v2's lean across nine horizons;
* a risk-versus-rank map, a factor radar (technical and SEC fundamentals, universe percentiles) and return
  correlations;
* a scoreboard of every frozen model, risk statistics, and option-implied volatility.

All of it comes from what the forward test logged, so it is instant. Then "Run the full model and trade plans" scores
each stock with v1.1 and shows its plan on a chart with the reason card. Its layout follows TriFrame's comparison view
(ranking, risk map, side-by-side distributions).

**Levels and reasons (new).** For every horizon the card states:
* the decision and every failed gate;
* the entry rule and maximum chase (E111);
* the stop and where it comes from: the setup's invalidation and rule ids, plus the 0.25 ATR buffer (X001, X011);
* the targets (X021) and the net reward-to-risk (E039, E149);
* the exit rules (X071) and the size;
* the weekly regime, daily setup and hourly trigger with rule titles from the owner's rulebooks;
* the indicators (RSI, MACD, SMAs, Bollinger, volume, ATR) and the model's probabilities;
* the company texts (news with tone, SEC filings), macro lines where available, and book or rulebook citations.

When the engine says NO_TRADE but a setup exists, the setup's own geometry is drawn **dashed and marked CONDITIONAL -
NOT APPROVED**, using the engine's rule-only path. The card adds the backtest evidence on such plans: in the audited
2020-22 backtest, rule-only plans had Sharpe -0.87 at 5D and -0.34 at 20D, against 0.53 for equal-weight buy-and-hold.
The engine itself is unchanged.

**What actually blocks v1.1's plans** (latest logged session, 2026-11-11):
* **1D and 2D:** direction skill is not validated (gate 3).
* **5D to 1M:** the frozen flags count as validated, with validation lower bounds 0.504-0.515. But the edge gate needs
  |P(UP) - P(DOWN)| >= 0.10: 2.6% of stocks reach it at 5D, 10% at 10D, 22% at 20D and 29% at 1M. The weekly,
  daily and hourly gates then decide.
* **Test years:** the direction AUROC on 2020-22 is 0.49-0.51 at every horizon (notebook section 12), so the small
  validation skill did not replicate.

**Risk rules (master specification section 13).** The engine's 11 gates (`trading/plan.py`, notebook section 15):
* data sufficiency, a calibrated forecast, validated direction skill, the minimum edge;
* weekly permission, a daily setup and an hourly trigger;
* a stop on the losing side, the first-obstacle target with net reward-to-risk of at least 1.5;
* risk-budget sizing capped by the maximum position, a time exit and a chase limit.

Account capital, risk per trade, maximum position, short selling and the cost scenario are user settings.

## 5. The end-to-end notebook

`notebooks/NEXUS_Alpha_End_to_End.ipynb` is the presentation copy of the project. It holds the code of every step
inline (`scripts/colab/make_end_to_end_notebook.py` builds it). It reads a compact input set staged to Drive by
`scripts/colab/stage_notebook_inputs.py`:
* the sample frame;
* the chart-embedding components, fitted on training rows;
* the news, SEC and rule features;
* the saved predictions of E01, E02, E03, E06, E08 and E14;
* the knowledge base.

Daily bars are read with a date filter, so no sealed row is ever loaded.

It covers, in order:
* setup and governance checks;
* coverage; labels and thresholds re-derived and checked; purged splits re-verified;
* the indicator formulas re-implemented and checked against the stored features;
* the class prior; logistic regression with a five-start **global-minimum certificate** (zero gradient, positive
  definite Hessian); LightGBM with **training, early-stopping and validation curves** and feature importance;
* the gated **multimodal fusion network** on the GPU (three seeds, **loss curves**, gate weights);
* the optimisation evidence: seed spread, the loss along the line between two seeds, and E06's 2-D landscape;
* **fine-tuning** ImageNet ConvNeXt-Tiny on charts drawn by the r1 renderer, on the GPU, with **loss curves**;
* temperature calibration with reliability diagrams and ECE;
* every metric by horizon (log loss, Brier, accuracy, balanced accuracy, macro-F1, AUROC, up-vs-down and sideways
  AUROC, ECE, rank IC), **confusion matrices** and ROC curves;
* month-block bootstrap intervals, the placebo and Holm;
* the comparison of every model, and a **reproduction check** of E03's committed numbers from the saved predictions;
* the risk engine re-implemented on real cases, the backtest, the RAG retrievers, and the forward test's hash chains
  verified from GitHub.

Run it on Colab Pro+ with an A100 (about 30-45 minutes), or with `QUICK = True` as a smoke test.

## 6. Can the results be optimised further?

**What is already optimal, and in what sense:**
* the logistic component sits at its certified global minimum (E09, and again in the notebook);
* LightGBM's parameters were tuned with Optuna on validation (E07), and boosting stops on the inner block;
* the network's learning rate, dropout and width were searched in E06; five seeds sit within 0.001 of each other on
  validation, and the 2-D landscape around the optimum is a smooth bowl;
* the blend weights of v1.1 and v2 were chosen on validation (E08, E14).

**Why more tuning now would not help.** `docs/TRIAL_LEDGER.md` counts up to 665 selection trials on 2017-2018.
Each further trial on those years makes the validation numbers look better and the model no better on new data. That
is the overfitting this project was built to avoid. The 2020-22 test years have been read many times too.

**Where an honest improvement could come from** (each needs a pre-registration in `docs/V3_RESEARCH.md` before it is
run):
1. FinBERT (or SEC-BERT) on top of F0, on a Colab GPU. This is the open E16 candidate.
2. Ranking objectives for the part that works (cross-sectional rank), judged by forward rank IC, not by validation
   log loss.
3. More forward sessions. The forward test is the only clean data: about 120 sessions decide v3_fund and v3_alpha.
4. The sealed 2023+ evaluation, once, after the final freeze.
