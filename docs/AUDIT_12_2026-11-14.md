# Audit 12 (2026-11-14): fundamentals, the text branch, and what "A tier" would take

The owner asked for:
- every weakness patched;
- financial metrics such as P/E and EBITDA added;
- the architecture and pipeline made more robust;
- an honest deep dive, as a professional quant researcher would do one.

Full authority was given.

## 1. What was found

**Financial metrics.** The project already had them:
- 31 point-in-time ratios, including earnings yield (inverse P/E), EBITDA/EV, ROIC, Piotroski F, Altman Z, accruals and
  SUE;
- 7 multiples: P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/S, P/B, P/FCF, market cap and EV.

They had been tested inside fitted models (E22 not kept; E23 kept as v3_fund). Three things were missing:
- published composite and forensic scores;
- a place in the dashboard to read them;
- a test of whether any of them predicts returns on its own.

## 2. What was built

**Extended fundamentals** (`src/nexus_alpha/fundamentals/extended.py`, tests in `tests/test_extended_fundamentals.py`).
Each formula was checked against a published or secondary source on the web before coding:
- Beneish M (eight indices, 1999 coefficients);
- Montier C (six signals);
- Novy-Marx gross profitability;
- Greenblatt's magic formula;
- QMJ-style quality (profitability, one-year growth, safety);
- the value composite;
- PEG, EV/EBIT, EBIT/EV, payout, cash ROA, FCF margin, ROCE, the DuPont parts, DSO, DIO, capex/D&A, R&D and SG&A
  intensity, cash conversion, quick and cash ratios.

Ohlson's O-score was left out on purpose: its size term needs a 1968 price index, and its coefficients degrade on recent
data.

**E30** (D-060, `docs/E30_PREREGISTRATION.md`, `docs/E30_RESULTS.md`). This is the test a factor researcher runs first:
the six published composites exactly as defined, no fitting, one shot, point in time, Holm across the six. The
pre-registration was committed before the run.

- **Result:** none replicates (164 monthly cross-sections, 2009-2022).
- **Closest:** gross profitability (IC +0.018, one-sided p 0.051) and Piotroski (+0.014, p 0.040). Neither survives
  Holm.
- **Value** is about zero.

The forward-only alternative was rejected with a power calculation: 120 sessions of 20-session returns hold about six
independent observations.

**Dashboard: tab P Fundamentals.**
- valuation tiles and score rings;
- a percentile radar against the universe and the industry;
- what drives the Beneish M-score;
- point-in-time history of multiples, margins and returns on capital;
- an industry peer map (EV/EBITDA against ROIC);
- the E30 result next to it all, so no score looks predictive.

**Live cross-section.** The nightly live cross-section also stores the extended metrics and composites, under an `x_`
prefix. The frozen v3_fund reads an explicit column list, which is unchanged.

**E29 made operational** (D-061, A-113):
- `news_on_forecast.py` runs the frozen v1.1 again, from the same cached bars, with the archived headlines. A headline
  is usable only if this machine had fetched it before the decision time. The log goes to `shadow/v1.1_news` with its
  own hash chain and is pushed before 09:00 New York.
- `e29_score.py` scores E29a and E29b every night, with E29b's pre-registered start condition coded and tested.

## 3. SWOT after this audit

**Strengths**
- Point-in-time discipline everywhere, and pre-registration before every result.
- A null result is published as a null.
- The forward test is hash-chained and public.
- Fundamentals coverage is now broad (31 ratios, 7 multiples, about 25 extended metrics, 6 composites).

**Weaknesses**
- Tag coverage (A-112).
- A survivor-only universe.
- Price-only historical returns.
- The forward test is still young (5 of about 120 sessions).
- One machine runs it.

**Opportunities**
- A wider tag map in the next model generation.
- Gross profitability and Piotroski as pre-registered inputs to that generation.
- E29b gives the text branch its first live test.
- A second runner removes the single point of failure.

**Threats**
- The edge shrinks year by year (A-111).
- Vendor changes (Yahoo, SEC tags).
- A missed night is lost for good (D-052).
- Over-reading small historical gains: E30 shows how easily a plausible signal fails a clean test.

## 4. What "A tier" would take

These are honest conditions, not code changes:
1. **A forward record.** At least 120 scored sessions with the ranking edge intact after costs: about six months of
   uninterrupted nightly runs. No code can shorten this.
2. **A point-in-time, survivorship-free universe** (delisted firms and historical index membership). That needs a paid
   data source such as CRSP/Compustat or Norgate; free sources do not provide it.
3. **Dividend-adjusted returns** and a realistic cost model (spread plus impact, by liquidity bucket).
4. **The sealed 2023+ test, opened once** by the owner, after the forward test, with the scorer as frozen today.
