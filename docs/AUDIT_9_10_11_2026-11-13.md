### 9.3 SWOT 1 (after audit 9)

**Strengths**
* Governance that held under two independent audits:
  * pre-registration, a trial ledger, hash-verified frozen models and hash-chained forward logs;
  * a sealed period never read;
  * `run_all --full` 10/10 with 197 tests.
* Reproducibility to the last decimal: labels (0 mismatches), E03 (1e-5), the SEC expert (1e-16), the logistic
  global-minimum certificate, and ChronoBERT parity (0.9998).
* Honest nulls: E28 not kept twice; the fine-tuned CNN worse than the prior; "no strategy beats buy-and-hold" stated
  everywhere.
* A real but modest signal that survives on 2020-22:
  * v1.1 beats the prior at 1D-5D after Holm;
  * rank IC 0.026-0.034 with intervals above zero at 2D-10D;
  * sideways AUROC about 0.59.

**Weaknesses**
* No direction skill out of sample, yet the engine's gate trusted the 2017-18 flags (A-101).
* The flagship vision branch adds nothing once indicators are present, and text adds less than the seed noise.
* The trading evaluation had design gaps (A-90..A-96), and no rule is profitable after costs.
* Seed noise is as large as most of the effects tested (A-84, A-97).

**Opportunities**
* The signal is about **volatility** (will it move) and **ranking**, not direction. That fits:
  * option and volatility strategies, which E26 already tests forward against implied volatility;
  * cost-aware, low-turnover long-short books.
* A leaner model (indicators + language, no charts) matched the full one on 2017-18, at a fraction of the cost and
  with fewer failure modes. It is a forward-test candidate only after pre-registration.
* The forward test is maturing toward 120 sessions; it is the only clean evidence.
* A live news feed would let the forward test exercise the text branch, which it never sees today.

**Threats**
* **Forward-test fragility.** Sleep, Windows Update, Yahoo staleness and the logon type (A-76) can each lose a
  session permanently.
* **Data drift.** News coverage moves by a factor of two between blocks (A-99); Yahoo and vendor bars differ.
* **Overfitting by reuse.** 681 selection trials on 2017-18, and 2020-22 read many times.
* **Users acting on directional plans that the evidence no longer supports** (now closed by A-101).
* **A public repository.** Every commit is public at the next 07:00 push.

### 11.4 Optimising the models: the decision

**No model was retrained, re-tuned or re-selected, and that is the optimisation.** Every network already stops on
held-out loss well before its epoch cap (13A shows the epochs per seed), so more epochs move along a rising curve.
The decision years 2017-18 have served about 681 selection trials, and 2020-22 has been read many times. A model
chosen on them now would look better on paper and be worth less.

**E29, revised.** Audit 8 proposed a forward-only E29: a ranking objective, a label-shift correction, ten seeds and a
recent-skill gate. Audits 9-11 change that proposal in three ways:
* **The recent-skill part is done as a rule.** It is D-057's replication gate, which can only close gates.
* **A ranking candidate adds little now.** The forward test already logs three rankers: v2's `rank_score`, v3_fund and
  v3_alpha. A dedicated ranker did not beat that baseline in E21.
* **The text branch cannot be tested forward yet.** Live forecasts have no news, so a "leaner" indicators + language
  model would run live as an indicators-only model, like v3_fund and v3_alpha.

**Recommendation:** fit nothing new now. Let the forward test reach 120 sessions, and let the news archive grow.
Then pre-register E29 as two things: a label-shift-corrected calibration of v1.1, and a forward test of the text
branch on the archived live news. Holm-adjust across them. This is for the owner to approve.
