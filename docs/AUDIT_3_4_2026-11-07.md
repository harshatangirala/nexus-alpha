## 2. Experiments

### E22 (fundamentals, universe ranks)

Not kept under the pre-registered rule. Full tables are in `docs/V3_RESEARCH.md`.
* **Probabilities:** no improvement (LightGBM worse at long horizons).
* **Cross-sectional ranking:** the logistic model's rank IC improved strongly, at swing and long horizons on 2017-18,
  and again on 2020-22.
* **Why dropped:** the within-date placebo also cleared the any-group bar on swing, by +0.00015.

### E23 (industry-neutral ranks)

**Kept** under the pre-registered rule: LightGBM log loss and rank IC, logistic rank IC, with no placebo passing.
* **Validation 2017-18:** LightGBM long-horizon log loss +0.0037 [+0.0020, +0.0059]; rank IC +0.054 [+0.022, +0.089].
* **2020-22 diagnostic:** only the swing ranking gains replicated (LightGBM +0.007 [+0.002, +0.012]; logistic +0.007
  [+0.002, +0.011]).
* **Deployment:** by the use clauses, `v3_fund` = LightGBM on F0 + industry-neutral fundamentals, refitted on
  2009-2018 and frozen. It is logged nightly; its forward promotion criterion was registered before any forecast
  (D-042).

**Defects found by audit 4.**
* **A-47 (High).** The vertical tab that a patch wrote into `run_daily.cmd` would have made the v3_fund step fail
  silently every night. `tests/test_shadow_runner.py` now guards the runner.
* **A-48 (High).** `latest_bundle()` treated any bundle without a `kind` field as a v1 stock bundle, so the new
  v3_fund bundle became the dashboard's default predictor and failed to load. The test suite caught it (two
  `test_inference` errors). A v1 bundle must now hold its preprocessor, and new fundamentals bundles carry
  `"kind": "fund"`; the frozen bundle itself is unchanged.
