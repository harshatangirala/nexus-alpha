# Audit 8 (2026-11-13): the Colab A100 run reviewed, vision vs NLP evaluated, and the 1-5 stock desk

**Request.** After the Colab Pro+ run of `NEXUS_Alpha_End_to_End_v2.ipynb` (2026-11-13, A100), the owner asked for:
1. A review of the executed results in depth, end to end, for loopholes and drawbacks, at industry standard.
2. Every evaluation metric for the computer-vision and NLP parts separately, whether vision, NLP or both together
   predict better, and entry, exit and stop-loss criteria as evaluation metrics.
3. A code cell at the bottom of the notebook that writes a full report of a run.
4. A Streamlit desk for 1 to 5 stocks with screenshot inputs (upload or paste) and 1-7 news articles per stock.
5. An answer: do the news, SEC filings and macro texts carry their date and time as an observation factor?
6. More epochs, fine-tuning or a different architecture, if the evidence calls for them.

**Standard.** As in audits 1-7: every number traces to a file; nothing may see the future; rules come before results;
every selection trial is counted; nothing fails silently. The sealed 2023+ period was not read. No frozen model,
freeze record, forward-test log or scheduled task was changed.

## Verdict

**The A100 run is clean and confirms the recorded results.** All 27 code cells ran in 28.9 minutes in full mode, with
no error. ChronoBERT re-encoded 68,558 filings on the GPU in 98 s with a median centred cosine of 0.99982 against the
CPU vectors (1st percentile 0.99151). The SEC expert refit matched the documented build to 1.1e-16. E28 was again NOT
KEPT (MM vs BASE -0.00007 [-0.00040, +0.00025]; vs placebo +0.00021 [+0.00003, +0.00038]).

**The run exposes four model-level weaknesses that no code bug explains** (A-83 to A-86 below): direction skill that
did not replicate, optimisation noise as large as the effects being tested, a calibration that does not transfer, and
a risk engine that almost never trades. They are properties of the evidence, not defects to patch. The right response
is a pre-registered, forward-only experiment (proposed below), not more epochs on reused validation years.

**Two real defects were found and fixed:** pasted news ignored the 5-day window the news branch was trained with
(A-82), and the article time fields accepted free text labelled UTC, inviting local times (A-87).

**Built:** notebook sections 13A (vision vs NLP vs indicators, every metric), 16A (entry, stop-loss and exit on every
modality's forecasts against random books) and 20 (a full report to Drive); the dashboard tab **Desk 1-5** with paste
support and timed texts.

**Reading.** Indicators carry most of the forecastable information, the charts carry some, and text alone carries
little (it exists for about two thirds of the rows: news on 39%, a filing on 52%). Together they are best, but the
language blocks add nothing that survives the validation test. Direction (up vs down) is near chance for every input;
what each input predicts is **whether** a stock moves and its cross-sectional rank.

Section 13A now trains every combination in the notebook (vision only, NLP only, indicators only, vision + NLP,
vision + indicators, indicators + NLP, all) and reports, for each: log loss, gain over the prior, Brier, accuracy,
balanced accuracy, macro-F1, AUROC, ECE, up-vs-down AUROC, sideways AUROC, rank IC at 5D and 20D, the coverage of the
predicted 10-90% return range and the share below the 10% quantile (the natural stop distance), on all rows and on
rows with text, plus paired increments with month-block intervals. Section 16A puts each combination's forecasts through
one fixed trading rule: entry at the next open, stop-loss 1.5 ATR, target 3 ATR, time exit, 10 and 20 bps costs; with
win rate, target and stop shares, expectancy in R with an interval, profit factor, MFE and MAE, the long and short legs,
and a permutation p against 300 random books. The quick local run checked the mechanics; **the numbers come from the
owner's next A100 run** (QUICK mode trains two epochs, so its numbers mean nothing).

## 4. More epochs, fine-tuning or a new architecture?

**More epochs: no.** Every network stops on its own held-out loss well before its epoch budget: the fusion seeds at
9-14 of 30 epochs; the fine-tuned CNN at epoch 2 of 4, after which its held-out loss rose. Training longer moves along
a curve that is already rising. The seeds land in different basins of nearly equal loss (barrier +0.0096), so the
optimiser is not the bottleneck: the signal is.

**More fine-tuning: no.** The fine-tuned chart CNN is worse than the class prior (section 10), as TriFrame found;
frozen ImageNet embeddings work better (vision only +0.0044 on test).

**A different architecture: only as a pre-registered, forward-only experiment.** The validation years have served up
to 681 selection trials (`docs/TRIAL_LEDGER.md`), and the test years were read by TriFrame. Choosing an architecture
on them again would select on noise (A-84 shows how large it is). A candidate design, for the owner to approve before
anything is fitted:

> **E29 (proposal).** Target what held out of sample: (1) a cross-sectional ranking objective (a listwise loss on each
> date's stocks) in place of the absolute three-class label; (2) a label-shift correction from trailing realised class
> shares (A-85); (3) ten seeds with a seed-level interval (A-84); (4) a direction gate that also requires the skill on
> the most recent 12 months (A-83). Fitted once on 2003-2018 with the current settings, frozen, and judged only by the
> forward test against v1.1 and `ranker_v3` with the existing promotion rule. No use of 2017-2022 for choices.

## 5. Do the texts carry their date and time?

Yes, at three levels, and the calendar date itself is deliberately never a feature (D-054: calendar time is a
look-ahead proxy).
* **Availability (all texts).** A text with a known minute counts 15 minutes after publication; a text with only a
  date counts from the end of that day in US Pacific time. A text that becomes available after the decision time
  (09:00 New York on the session after the as-of close) is never read.
* **Features (company news).** The news branch reads the hours since the latest article, the share of articles with a
  minute timestamp, the number of articles and clusters within the 5-day window, tone and text components.
* **SEC filings** carry EDGAR's acceptance time: days since the last 8-K, 10-Q and 10-K, 8-K counts over 7 and 30 days,
  earnings in the last 3 days, and whether the filing came after the close.
* **Macro** comes from ALFRED real-time vintages: the value as it was known that day, and its age in days.

Desk 1-5 makes this visible: every text gets date, time, time zone and "time known"; the chronology chart places it
against the as-of close, the 5-day window and the decision time, and marks whether the model reads it.
